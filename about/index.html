About Me

I started as an illustrator, and that's where my eye for detail comes from. Ten years at a digital agency taught me to adapt: many clients, many teams, and the same recurring problems, which is where I started thinking in systems. At Ripio, I led design across several squads, prioritizing accessibility and usability. At Producers Trust I worked as an individual contributor with an international team. At Relyance AI I built a design system from scratch. Along the way, one thing stayed constant: I design for people who just need the product to work. I'm comfortable working solo or inside teams, small or large, and I put accessibility and usability at the center of every decision, with WCAG AA as my baseline.
